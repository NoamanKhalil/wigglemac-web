# Wiggle does not ask you to make an account

Wiggle does not ask you to make an account, and the app does not ship with analytics. This website does not sign you in. A visit sends a page view to Google Analytics. The only message we receive ourselves is an email you send.

By Noaman · October 20, 2026
## Key points

- No Wiggle account.
- No analytics in the Mac app.
- This website does not sign you in.
- An email you send to hello@jabble.si is the only message you write to us from here. A page view also goes to Google Analytics.

## What a visit stores
Reading these pages does not create a profile. There is no score, and there is no manager to send one to. That last idea is only on the parody page.

Privacy (/privacy.html) is the short policy. Your data (/your-data.html) says what this website itself keeps. A page view goes to Google Analytics, and an email you choose to write comes to us.

## No profile
Loading a page does not create a Wiggle profile. The site sends a page view to Google Analytics. There is no cookie banner, and the site does not ask you to sign in.

## Email
Early access is an email to hello@jabble.si (mailto:hello@jabble.si). That message is the one you wrote. It is not a form that builds a score.

The parody page’s “disengagement event” sent to your manager is fiction.
